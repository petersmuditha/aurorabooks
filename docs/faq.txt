AURORA BOOKS FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ABOUT AURORA BOOKS
Aurora Books is an online bookstore based in Italy.
We sell books in Italian, English, French and Spanish.
Our catalog includes fiction, non fiction, children books, academic texts and rare editions.
We ship across all Europe and offer free shipping on orders over 30 euro.

OPENING HOURS OF CUSTOMER SERVICE
Our customer service is available from Monday to Friday from 9 to 18.
On Saturday from 10 to 14.
We are closed on Sunday and on national holidays.
You can contact us by email at support at aurorabooks dot it.

HOW TO PLACE AN ORDER
You can order in three ways.
First, browse our online catalog on the website and add books to your cart.
Second, call our customer service and place the order by phone.
Third, send an email with the list of books you want to purchase.
After ordering, you will receive a confirmation email with the order number and tracking link.

SHIPPING AND DELIVERY
Standard shipping within Italy takes 3 to 5 working days and costs 4 euro.
Express shipping within Italy takes 1 to 2 working days and costs 9 euro.
Shipping to other European countries takes 5 to 10 working days and costs 12 euro.
Shipping is free for orders over 30 euro within Italy.
We ship with the courier GLS and BRT.
Tracking information is provided in the confirmation email.

PAYMENT METHODS
We accept credit cards Visa Mastercard American Express.
We accept PayPal and bank transfers.
We accept cash on delivery only within Italy with an extra fee of 3 euro.
All payments are processed securely through Stripe.
We do not store credit card information on our servers.

RETURNS AND REFUNDS
You can return a book within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
The book must be in its original condition, with no marks or damage.
To start a return, contact support at aurorabooks dot it with your order number.
We will send you a prepaid return label.
Refunds are processed within 5 working days after we receive the book.

BOOK AVAILABILITY
If a book is not in stock, you can request a notification when it becomes available.
We can also order books from publishers that are not in our catalog.
Special orders take 2 to 4 weeks to arrive.
For rare editions, please contact us directly for a quote.

GIFT CARDS
Gift cards are available for any amount starting from 20 euro.
You can buy them online on our website.
Gift cards are valid for 24 months from the date of purchase.
Gift cards can be used for any purchase including shipping.
Gift cards are not refundable but can be transferred to another person.

LOYALTY PROGRAM
Aurora Readers is our free loyalty program.
You earn 1 point for every 5 euro spent.
Every 100 points give you a 5 euro discount on your next order.
Members receive early access to sales and new releases.
You can subscribe to Aurora Readers directly on our website.

BOOK CLUB AND EVENTS
We organize a monthly online book club.
Meetings are held on the last Thursday of every month at 20.
Participation is free and open to all readers.
We also host author interviews and reading events.
Subscribe to our newsletter to receive the calendar.
